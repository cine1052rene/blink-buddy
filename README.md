# Blink Buddy

A SPECS Lens that sits beside your screen and helps you blink and rest your eyes while you focus.
Built for **Learn AR with SPECS** (Devpost) as a **self-hackathon**: the idea came up at **04:38 KST on Nov 10, 2026**, while submitting my other entry ([Exit Thread](https://github.com/cine1052rene/exit-thread)), and everything here was planned, built and submitted after that.

## What it does
- **A small firefly** floats at the edge of your view, beside your monitor, never in the middle of your task.
- **Blink with me:** every few minutes it slowly closes its eyes three times, and you blink with it. Pinch it to log the round.
- **20-20-20 break:** every 20 minutes it flies to your real window. Look far away for 20 seconds, then follow a slow figure-eight with your eyes.
- **Moving? It hides.** If you get up and walk, it fades away and pauses. Never use it while driving.

## Why glasses, and why a real window
A phone reminder pulls your eyes to another near screen. On glasses the cue sits quietly in the corner of the view you already have. A virtual "distant mountain" would not rest your eyes, because the display focus is fixed at about 1 m. Only a real, far object lets your focusing muscles relax, so the Lens sends you to the real world.

## Simulated in this preview (honest list)
- SPECS has **no eye-facing sensor**: blinks, pupil size and focus are never measured. Cues are time-based, and you pinch to say "done".
- The window is a stand-in, and the 20-minute timer is sped up for the demo.
- The demo video uses a preview-only scripted sequence (`demoAutoPlay`), labelled on screen.
- Comfort over long sessions and battery are unproven.

This is a habit helper, not a medical device. Small study on 20-20-20 reminders: Talens-Estarelles et al., *Contact Lens and Anterior Eye*, 2022.
